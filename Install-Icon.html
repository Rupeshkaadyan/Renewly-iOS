Renewly App Icon

Designed for Renewly — aurora gradient + renew mark.
Click below to render the 1024×1024 PNG, then drag it into Xcode.


  1. Click the download button — it renders a true 1024×1024 PNG and saves it to Downloads.
  2. In Xcode, drag that PNG onto the AppIcon well in Assets.xcassets
    (or drop it into Renewly/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/ in Finder).
  3. Rebuild — both warnings disappear and the new icon shows on the home screen.
